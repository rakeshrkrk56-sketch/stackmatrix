## How the Slack + Calendly integration works

Install the Calendly app from the Slack App Directory and connect your Calendly account. You can share your scheduling links in channels and DMs with meeting previews, and the app messages you in Slack whenever a meeting is scheduled, rescheduled or cancelled.

[Setup guide: Calendly help: Calendly + Slack ↗](https://calendly.com/help/calendly-slack)

### How do you set up Slack + Calendly?

1. Sign in to Slack and open the Slack app directory.
2. Find Calendly, select Add to Slack and then Allow.
3. Select Connect to Calendly, then Open Slack to return to the app. A welcome message from the Calendly app confirms the setup.
4. Teammates connect their own accounts: in Slack, go to Apps, select + and search for Calendly, open its Home page and select Connect.
5. To share a link, type /Calendly in a channel or direct message, select Share an event type with Calendly and choose the event type. Sharing doesn't work inside threads.

Steps follow [Calendly's setup guide ↗](https://calendly.com/help/calendly-slack), read Nov 10, 2026.

**Limitations:** Each person connects their own Calendly account after a workspace admin installs the app, and your company can block app installation. Notifications arrive in the Calendly app's Messages tab, not in a channel.

## Slack + Calendly integration: frequently asked questions

### What are the minimum plan tiers required for the Slack + Calendly integration?

To unlock the native integration, Slack must be on the 'Free' plan or higher and Calendly must be on the 'Free' plan or higher. Those are each tool's cheapest plan with API access (Slack 'Free' at $0/mo and Calendly 'Free' at $0/mo for 10 seats), so the integration doesn't force an upgrade.

### How much does the Slack + Calendly stack cost per month for a 10-person team?

At 10 seats with the required tiers (Slack 'Free' + Calendly 'Free'), the stack costs $0.00 per month.

### Is the Slack + Calendly integration native, and would Make or Zapier be cheaper?

The Slack + Calendly connection is native — no third-party middleware subscription is required. Make would cost about $10.59/month on the cheapest API plans, more than the native route, so it only makes sense if you already use it.

### What are the limitations of the Slack + Calendly integration?

Each person connects their own Calendly account after a workspace admin installs the app, and your company can block app installation. Notifications arrive in the Calendly app's Messages tab, not in a channel.
