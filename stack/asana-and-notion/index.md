## How the Asana + Notion integration works

**Does Asana integrate with Notion?** Yes, in a limited way. Notion makes an Asana connection: paste an Asana link to see a live preview of the task, or paste it as a database to bring a project's tasks into Notion. Notion notes that this isn't a two-way sync.

Paste an Asana link and choose Paste as preview, or type /asana, to show the task's details in a Notion page. Pasting a project or task link as a database creates a Notion database with Asana properties that fills with the tasks; Notion says the data is duplicated into Notion at the moment of import and won't sync any changes.

### How do you set up Asana + Notion?

1. Paste an Asana link into a Notion page and select Paste as preview, or type /asana to paste links from a menu.
2. The first time, authorize the Asana connection when Notion prompts you.
3. To bring tasks into a database, copy an Asana project or task link, paste it into Notion and select Paste as database.

Steps follow [Notion's setup guide ↗](https://www.notion.com/integrations/asana), read Nov 12, 2026.

**Limitations:** It's one-way, and a database made from Asana is a one-time copy: Notion says the data won't sync any changes after the import. Notion's page names no plan for either tool, so this page uses Asana Starter and Notion Plus, the cheapest plans of each that this site prices.

## Asana + Notion integration: frequently asked questions

### What are the minimum plan tiers required for the Asana + Notion integration?

To unlock the native integration, Asana must be on the 'Starter' plan or higher and Notion must be on the 'Plus' plan or higher. Those are each tool's cheapest plan with API access (Asana 'Starter' at $134.90/mo and Notion 'Plus' at $120/mo for 10 seats), so the integration doesn't force an upgrade.

### How much does the Asana + Notion stack cost per month for a 10-person team?

At 10 seats with the required tiers (Asana 'Starter' + Notion 'Plus'), the stack costs $254.90 per month.

### Is the Asana + Notion integration native, and would Make or Zapier be cheaper?

The Asana + Notion connection is native — no third-party middleware subscription is required. Make would cost about $265.49/month on the cheapest API plans, more than the native route, so it only makes sense if you already use it.

### What are the limitations of the Asana + Notion integration?

It's one-way, and a database made from Asana is a one-time copy: Notion says the data won't sync any changes after the import. Notion's page names no plan for either tool, so this page uses Asana Starter and Notion Plus, the cheapest plans of each that this site prices.
