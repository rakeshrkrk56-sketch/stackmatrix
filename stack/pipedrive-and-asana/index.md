## How the Pipedrive + Asana integration works

**Does Pipedrive integrate with Asana?** Yes. Pipedrive builds an Asana integration that creates Asana tasks or projects from Pipedrive deals. It runs one way, from Pipedrive to Asana.

A rule sets the trigger, a deal won, lost or moved to a stage you choose in a pipeline, and what to send: the deal title, value, contact details, pinned notes and attachments. It then creates a task in an Asana project, optionally assigned to someone, or a new project. Pipedrive's Automations add four ready-made Asana templates, one of them for new leads.

### How do you set up Pipedrive + Asana?

1. In the Pipedrive Marketplace, open the Asana integration, click Install now (or Authorize), choose who can use it and click Next.
2. Review the permissions and click Allow and install, then sign in to Asana and click Allow.
3. Open the Account menu > Tools and apps > Asana, and choose the trigger (deal won, lost or moved to a stage) and the pipeline.
4. Choose the deal details to send and whether to create a task or a project, pick the Asana workspace and project, and click Save changes.

Steps follow [Pipedrive's setup guide ↗](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/pipedrive-integration-asana), read Nov 12, 2026.

**Limitations:** It's one-way, from Pipedrive to Asana; each admin user can create one rule, and projects can't carry attachments. Pipedrive's Asana templates in Automations need its Growth plan or higher; the article names no plan for the basic rule, so this page models Pipedrive Lite. Asana is modeled at Starter, the cheapest Asana plan this site prices.

## Pipedrive + Asana integration: frequently asked questions

### What are the minimum plan tiers required for the Pipedrive + Asana integration?

To unlock the native integration, Pipedrive must be on the 'Lite' plan or higher and Asana must be on the 'Starter' plan or higher. Those are each tool's cheapest plan with API access (Pipedrive 'Lite' at $140/mo and Asana 'Starter' at $134.90/mo for 10 seats), so the integration doesn't force an upgrade.

### How much does the Pipedrive + Asana stack cost per month for a 10-person team?

At 10 seats with the required tiers (Pipedrive 'Lite' + Asana 'Starter'), the stack costs $274.90 per month.

### Is the Pipedrive + Asana integration native, and would Make or Zapier be cheaper?

The Pipedrive + Asana connection is native — no third-party middleware subscription is required. Make would cost about $285.49/month on the cheapest API plans, more than the native route, so it only makes sense if you already use it.
