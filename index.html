B2B SaaS integration cost methodology & FAQ

Every true-cost figure on StackMatrix is built the same way: we verify per-seat pricing for each plan tier, identify exactly which tier unlocks API access and webhooks, and price middleware alternatives from published plans. Below are the hidden-cost patterns we see most often.

What are hidden API tier costs?

Hidden API tier costs are the extra subscription fees you pay because an integration only works on a higher plan. Many vendors gate API access, webhooks, or custom integrations behind Professional or Enterprise tiers — so the "integration" that looked free on the Starter plan can force a 3–5x price jump once you actually connect your tools.

What is a webhook paywall?

A webhook paywall is when a SaaS vendor restricts outgoing webhooks — the triggers that let tools talk to each other in real time — to upper-tier plans. Without webhook access, integrations fall back to slow polling or stop working entirely.

Native vs middleware: which has lower total cost of ownership?

Native integrations carry no middleware fee but may require expensive plan tiers on one or both tools. Middleware such as Zapier or Make adds a monthly fee but lets you stay on cheaper tiers. Our calculator compares both totals at your team size so you can see which TCO is actually lower.

What are per-seat pricing traps?

Per-seat pricing traps happen when an integration forces every user onto a higher tier — not just the people who need it. A $20/seat jump across a 50-person team is $12,000/year, even if only five people use the connected workflow.
