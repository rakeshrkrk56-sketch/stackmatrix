## Which ActiveCampaign integrations force a plan upgrade?

2 of the 10 integrations need a more expensive ActiveCampaign plan than Starter, ActiveCampaign's cheapest plan with API access. The extra ActiveCampaign cost at 10 seats: ActiveCampaign + Slack needs Plus (+$34.00/mo); ActiveCampaign + Stripe needs Plus (+$34.00/mo). That gap is the hidden cost: you pay it every month even if you only wanted the integration. Each pair's "Hidden Cost Trap" box shows the full breakdown.

## Which ActiveCampaign integrations need Make or Zapier?

6 of the 10 have no native connector and run through a third-party service: ClickUp + ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Xero + ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Asana + ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Monday.com + ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Freshdesk + ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Intercom + ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/mo). Connector fees are the monthly-billing price; the calculators use the lower annual-billing price (Make Core $9, Zapier Professional $19.99, Data Mapper $12.75) when you switch on annual billing. Real usage can push Make or Zapier onto a bigger plan, so check your expected volume.

## ActiveCampaign integration costs: frequently asked questions

### Which ActiveCampaign plan do I need for integrations?

Of the 10 ActiveCampaign integrations in this index, 8 work on ActiveCampaign's Starter plan. The rest need a higher plan: Slack needs ActiveCampaign Plus; Stripe needs ActiveCampaign Plus. Each pair's calculator shows the exact minimum plan on both sides.

### Which ActiveCampaign integrations need Zapier or Make?

6 of the 10 have no native connector: ClickUp (Make Core, $10.59/month); Xero (Make Core, $10.59/month); Asana (Make Core, $10.59/month); Monday.com (Make Core, $10.59/month); Freshdesk (Make Core, $10.59/month); Intercom (Make Core, $10.59/month).

### How much does a ActiveCampaign integration cost for a 10-person team?

Including both tools' minimum plans and any connector fee, the 10-seat monthly totals range from $25.59 (ClickUp + ActiveCampaign) to $415.59 (Intercom + ActiveCampaign), at monthly-billing prices.
