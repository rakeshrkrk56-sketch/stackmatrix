## Which Calendly integrations force a plan upgrade?

8 of the 10 integrations need a more expensive Calendly plan than Free, Calendly's cheapest plan with API access. The extra Calendly cost at 10 seats: Salesforce + Calendly needs Teams (+$200.00/mo); HubSpot + Calendly needs Standard (+$120.00/mo); Mailchimp + Calendly needs Standard (+$120.00/mo); Stripe + Calendly needs Standard (+$120.00/mo); ActiveCampaign + Calendly needs Standard (+$120.00/mo); Xero + Calendly needs Standard (+$120.00/mo); Notion + Calendly needs Standard (+$120.00/mo); Pipedrive + Calendly needs Standard (+$120.00/mo). That gap is the hidden cost: you pay it every month even if you only wanted the integration. Each pair's "Hidden Cost Trap" box shows the full breakdown.

## Which Calendly integrations need Make or Zapier?

3 of the 10 have no native connector and run through a third-party service: Xero + Calendly (Zapier Professional, $29.99/mo); Notion + Calendly (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Pipedrive + Calendly (Make Core, $10.59/mo). Connector fees are the monthly-billing price; the calculators use the lower annual-billing price (Make Core $9, Zapier Professional $19.99, Data Mapper $12.75) when you switch on annual billing. Real usage can push Make or Zapier onto a bigger plan, so check your expected volume.

## Calendly integration costs: frequently asked questions

### Which Calendly plan do I need for integrations?

Of the 10 Calendly integrations in this index, 7 work on Calendly's Standard plan. The rest need a higher plan: Slack needs Calendly Free; Intercom needs Calendly Free; Salesforce Sales Cloud needs Calendly Teams. Each pair's calculator shows the exact minimum plan on both sides.

### Which Calendly integrations need Zapier or Make?

3 of the 10 have no native connector: Xero (Zapier Professional, $29.99/month); Notion (Make Core, $10.59/month); Pipedrive (Make Core, $10.59/month).

### How much does a Calendly integration cost for a 10-person team?

Including both tools' minimum plans and any connector fee, the 10-seat monthly totals range from $0.00 (Slack + Calendly) to $1,850.00 (Salesforce + Calendly), at monthly-billing prices.
