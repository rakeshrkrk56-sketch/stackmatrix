## Which Asana integrations force a plan upgrade?

1 of the 10 integrations needs a more expensive Asana plan than Starter, Asana's cheapest plan with API access. The extra Asana cost at 10 seats: Asana + Salesforce needs Advanced (+$170.00/mo). That gap is the hidden cost: you pay it every month even if you only wanted the integration. Each pair's "Hidden Cost Trap" box shows the full breakdown. The [Integration Tax 2026 report](https://stackcostmatrix.com/integration-tax-2026/) adds these costs up across all 90 pairs in the index.

## Which Asana integrations need Make or Zapier?

4 of the 10 have no native connector and run through a third-party service: Asana + ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Xero + Asana (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Asana + Mailchimp (Zapier Professional, $29.99/mo); Asana + QuickBooks (Make Core, $10.59/mo). Connector fees are the monthly-billing price; the calculators use the lower annual-billing price (Make Core $9, Zapier Professional $19.99, Data Mapper $12.75) when you switch on annual billing. Real usage can push Make or Zapier onto a bigger plan, so check your expected volume.

## Asana integration costs: frequently asked questions

### Which Asana plan do I need for integrations?

Of the 10 Asana integrations in this index, 9 work on Asana's Starter plan. The rest need a higher plan: Salesforce Sales Cloud needs Asana Advanced. Each pair's calculator shows the exact minimum plan on both sides.

### Which Asana integrations need Zapier or Make?

4 of the 10 have no native connector: ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/month); Xero (Make Core, $10.59/month); Mailchimp (Zapier Professional, $29.99/month); QuickBooks Online (Make Core, $10.59/month).

### How much does a Asana integration cost for a 10-person team?

Including both tools' minimum plans and any connector fee, the 10-seat monthly totals range from $134.90 (Asana + Slack) to $1,554.90 (Asana + Salesforce), at monthly-billing prices.
