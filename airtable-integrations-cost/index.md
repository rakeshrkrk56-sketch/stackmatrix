## Which Airtable integrations force a plan upgrade?

5 of the 8 integrations need a more expensive Airtable plan than Free, Airtable's cheapest plan with API access. The extra Airtable cost at 10 seats: Zendesk + Airtable needs Business (+$540.00/mo); Salesforce + Airtable needs Business (+$540.00/mo); Airtable + Stripe needs Team (+$240.00/mo); Mailchimp + Airtable needs Team (+$240.00/mo); Airtable + QuickBooks needs Team (+$240.00/mo). That gap is the hidden cost: you pay it every month even if you only wanted the integration. Each pair's "Hidden Cost Trap" box shows the full breakdown.

## Which Airtable integrations need Make or Zapier?

4 of the 8 have no native connector and run through a third-party service: Xero + Airtable (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Airtable + Stripe (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Mailchimp + Airtable (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Airtable + QuickBooks (Make Core, $10.59/mo). Connector fees are the monthly-billing price; the calculators use the lower annual-billing price (Make Core $9, Zapier Professional $19.99, Data Mapper $12.75) when you switch on annual billing. Real usage can push Make or Zapier onto a bigger plan, so check your expected volume.

## Airtable integration costs: frequently asked questions

### Which Airtable plan do I need for integrations?

Of the 8 Airtable integrations in this index, 3 work on Airtable's Free plan. The rest need a higher plan: Stripe needs Airtable Team; Mailchimp needs Airtable Team; QuickBooks Online needs Airtable Team; Zendesk needs Airtable Business; Salesforce Sales Cloud needs Airtable Business. Each pair's calculator shows the exact minimum plan on both sides.

### Which Airtable integrations need Zapier or Make?

4 of the 8 have no native connector: Xero (Make Core, $10.59/month); Stripe (Make Core, $10.59/month); Mailchimp (Make Core, $10.59/month); QuickBooks Online (Make Core, $10.59/month).

### How much does a Airtable integration cost for a 10-person team?

Including both tools' minimum plans and any connector fee, the 10-seat monthly totals range from $0.00 (Slack + Airtable) to $1,790.00 (Salesforce + Airtable), at monthly-billing prices.
