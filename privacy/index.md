# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

## 1. What we collect

Nothing. StackCostMatrix is a static informational website. There are no accounts, forms or sign-ups, and we don't collect names, email addresses or payment details. The cost calculator and the integration finder run entirely in your browser, so the numbers you enter never leave your device.

## 2. Cookies & analytics

We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and we don't store anything in your browser. That's why there's no cookie banner: there's nothing to consent to. If we ever add analytics or ads, we'll update this page first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

## 3. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser requests a page and may keep it in its logs for security and to run the service. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 4. Images from other sites

The badges in the footer (Startup Fame, Fazier and SaaSHub) are images loaded from those sites, so they receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads, as any website you visit would. They set no cookies through our pages that we're aware of.

## 5. Outbound and affiliate links

Links to vendors open their own websites, which have their own privacy policies. Links through our /go/ pages currently take you straight to the vendor's pricing page with no tracking parameters. Some buttons are marked as affiliate links because we may join vendors' referral programs; if we do, the vendor or its affiliate network may set its own cookies on its site to credit the referral, and we'll say so here.

## 6. Email

If you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply and to fix any correction you report. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your email.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions, email [admin@stackcostmatrix.com](mailto:admin@stackcostmatrix.com).
