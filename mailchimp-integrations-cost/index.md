## Which Mailchimp integrations force a plan upgrade?

4 of the 10 integrations need a more expensive Mailchimp plan than Free, Mailchimp's cheapest plan with API access. The extra Mailchimp cost at 10 seats: HubSpot + Mailchimp needs Standard (+$20.00/mo); Salesforce + Mailchimp needs Standard (+$20.00/mo); Asana + Mailchimp needs Essentials (+$13.00/mo); Mailchimp + Airtable needs Essentials (+$13.00/mo). That gap is the hidden cost: you pay it every month even if you only wanted the integration. Each pair's "Hidden Cost Trap" box shows the full breakdown.

## Which Mailchimp integrations need Make or Zapier?

3 of the 10 have no native connector and run through a third-party service: Stripe + Mailchimp (Data Mapper for Mailchimp, $15.00/mo); Asana + Mailchimp (Zapier Professional, $29.99/mo); Mailchimp + Airtable (Make Core, $10.59/mo). Connector fees are the monthly-billing price; the calculators use the lower annual-billing price (Make Core $9, Zapier Professional $19.99, Data Mapper $12.75) when you switch on annual billing. Real usage can push Make or Zapier onto a bigger plan, so check your expected volume.

## Which Mailchimp plans include API access and webhooks?

On the plans these integrations need: Essentials (API access: yes; webhooks: no); Standard (API access: yes; webhooks: yes); Free (API access: yes; webhooks: no). API and webhook facts are listed for the plans that at least one integration below requires, with the date each was checked against Mailchimp's documentation. Mailchimp prices are starting prices for up to 500 contacts; the price rises with your contact count, not seats. ## Mailchimp integration costs: frequently asked questions

### Which Mailchimp plan do I need for integrations?

Of the 10 Mailchimp integrations in this index, 2 work on Mailchimp's Essentials plan. The rest need a higher plan: Slack needs Mailchimp Free; Stripe needs Mailchimp Free; HubSpot needs Mailchimp Standard; QuickBooks Online needs Mailchimp Free; Calendly needs Mailchimp Free; Pipedrive needs Mailchimp Free; Intercom needs Mailchimp Free; Salesforce Sales Cloud needs Mailchimp Standard. Each pair's calculator shows the exact minimum plan on both sides.

### Which Mailchimp integrations need Zapier or Make?

3 of the 10 have no native connector: Stripe (Data Mapper for Mailchimp, $15.00/month); Asana (Zapier Professional, $29.99/month); Airtable (Make Core, $10.59/month).

### How much does a Mailchimp integration cost for a 10-person team?

Including both tools' minimum plans and any connector fee, the 10-seat monthly totals range from $0.00 (Mailchimp + Slack) to $1,270.00 (Salesforce + Mailchimp), at monthly-billing prices.
