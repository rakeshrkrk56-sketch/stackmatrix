The Integration Tax 2026: What Connecting SaaS Tools Really Costs

StackCostMatrix · Published Nov 3, 2026 · Data checked against vendor docs Nov 3, 2026 · Updated Nov 5, 2026: HubSpot Professional repriced at HubSpot's per-seat price (see Limitations)

We priced 55 of the most common B2B software integrations at the plans each one actually requires, then compared that with the cheapest plans that still include API access. The difference, plus any connector fee for Make or Zapier, is the integration tax: what you pay every month only because two tools need to talk to each other.

Key findings

Plan upgrades, not connectors, are the real cost. Forced upgrades make up 93% of the total integration tax, and across all 55 stacks the integration tax is 20% of what they cost in total. Connector fees are small by comparison: $10.59, $15, $29.99 a month at monthly billing.

The tax is concentrated. The three most expensive integrations account for 38% of all the integration tax in the index. HubSpot + Asana and HubSpot + Salesforce each cost $500 a month extra because they need a HubSpot Professional subscription: Smart CRM Professional, the cheapest one, is $50 per seat a month (compare the Professional plans).

Calendly's webhook gate is the most common trigger. 6 integrations need Calendly Standard, because Calendly's webhooks require a paid plan; that's $120 a month for 10 seats.

26 of 55 integrations cost nothing extra. They work on each tool's cheapest plan with API access and need no connector, so connecting them is free on top of the plans you already pay for.

Methodology

Integration tax = (each tool's minimum plan for the integration − that tool's cheapest plan with API access) + the connector fee, for a 10-seat team at monthly-billing list prices. The report covers the 55 integrations in the index when it was published on Nov 3, 2026; pairs added since then aren't included, so the figures stay comparable. Minimum plans, connector fees and prices come from those pair calculators; every figure there links to the vendor's pricing page, help center or developer docs and was checked on Nov 3, 2026.

Baseline plans (cheapest plan with API access, 10 seats): ActiveCampaign Starter ($15/mo); Airtable Free ($0/mo); Asana Starter ($134.90/mo); Calendly Free ($0/mo); ClickUp Free ($0/mo); Freshdesk Growth ($230/mo); HubSpot Free ($0/mo); Intercom Essential ($390/mo); Mailchimp Free ($0/mo); Monday.com Standard ($140/mo); Notion Plus ($120/mo); Pipedrive Lite ($140/mo); QuickBooks Simple Start ($38/mo); Salesforce Professional ($1,250/mo); Slack Free ($0/mo); Stripe Standard ($0/mo); Xero Early ($27/mo); Zendesk Suite Team ($690/mo).

Limitations. HubSpot Professional is modeled as Smart CRM Professional ($50 per seat a month, $45 on annual billing), the cheapest subscription that unlocks the Salesforce connector and the Asana workflow action; other Professional Hubs cost more. Make Core's $10.59 monthly price comes from third-party pricing reviews, because make.com blocks automated checks. Salesforce's baseline includes the $25/user API add-on that Professional needs. Stripe has no plan fee, so its baseline is $0. 5 integrations include an upgrade that rests on a plan requirement we haven't confirmed yet (Salesforce + Slack, Mailchimp + Airtable, ActiveCampaign + Pipedrive, HubSpot + ClickUp, HubSpot + Mailchimp), worth $803 a month in total; counting only confirmed requirements, 15 of 55 integrations force an upgrade instead of 18. Usage-based charges (Zapier tasks, Make credits, API overages) aren't included.

Why the headline is split. Integrations that force a plan upgrade and those that only need a connector differ by an order of magnitude, so a single median across both swings between the two groups whenever one row changes. The figures are therefore reported per group: the range covers the middle half of the integrations in each group.

Frequently asked questions

What is the integration tax?

It's the extra monthly cost of connecting two SaaS tools: any plan upgrade an integration forces above each tool's cheapest plan with API access, plus any connector fee for a service such as Make or Zapier. Across 55 popular integrations, 29 have one.

How much does a SaaS integration cost per month?

It depends on whether the integration forces a plan upgrade. For a 10-person team, the 18 integrations here that force one usually add $120 to $251 a month (median $141.50), while the 11 that only need a connector add $10.59 to $29.99. 26 of 55 integrations cost nothing extra, and the most expensive, HubSpot + Asana and HubSpot + Salesforce, cost $500.00 a month.

Is it cheaper to use Zapier or Make than a native integration?

It can be. Connector fees in this index are $10.59, $15, $29.99 a month, while forced plan upgrades make up 93% of the total integration tax, so when a native connector needs a higher plan and the middleware route works on a cheaper one, middleware usually wins. Each pair's calculator compares the native and middleware totals at your team size.
