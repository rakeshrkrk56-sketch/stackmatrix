## Key findings

**Plan upgrades, not connectors, are the real cost.** Forced upgrades make up 94% of the total integration tax, and across all 90 stacks the integration tax is 18% of what they cost in total. Connector fees are small by comparison: $10.59, $15, $29.99 a month at monthly billing.

**The tax is concentrated.** The three most expensive integrations account for 25% of all the integration tax in the index. Intercom + Salesforce and Salesforce + Calendly each cost $600 a month extra: Intercom + Salesforce needs Intercom Advanced; Salesforce + Calendly needs Salesforce Enterprise and Calendly Teams. The integrations that need a HubSpot Professional subscription add $500: Smart CRM Professional, the cheapest one, is $50 per seat a month ([compare the Professional plans](https://stackcostmatrix.com/hubspot-professional-integrations/)).

**Calendly's paid plans are the most common trigger.** 8 integrations need a paid Calendly plan (Standard or Teams), because Calendly's webhooks, which connectors rely on, and several of its own integrations aren't on the free plan; that's at least $120 a month for 10 seats.

**37 of 90 integrations cost nothing extra.** They work on each tool's cheapest plan with API access and need no connector, so connecting them is free on top of the plans you already pay for.

## Three kinds of integration cost

“What does a SaaS integration cost?” can mean three different bills. This report measures the first two, because they're the ones a buyer of these tools actually meets.

1. **Plan upgrades (measured here).** The integration only works on a higher plan of one of the tools. That's 94% of the integration tax in this index, typically $120 to $320 a month for 10 seats when an upgrade is forced.
2. **Connector fees (measured here).** There's no native app, so you pay for Make, Zapier or a similar service: $10.59 to $29.99 a month at list price, more at high volumes.
3. **Building it yourself (not measured here).** If no native app or connector fits, someone writes and maintains a custom integration against both APIs. We don't measure that. For scale, Albato, an integration-platform vendor and so not a neutral source, cites Chift's cost analysis for roughly $10,000 to $22,000 in the first year for one in-house API integration, development plus maintenance ([Albato ↗](https://albato.com/blog/publications/embedded-ipaas-roi), read Nov 10, 2026).

## Methodology

**Integration tax** = (each tool's minimum plan for the integration − that tool's cheapest plan with API access) + the connector fee, for a 10-seat team at monthly-billing list prices (ActiveCampaign, Pipedrive and Salesforce publish annual-billing rates only, so those rates are used). The report covers all 90 integrations in the index on Nov 14, 2026: the 55 it was published with on Nov 3 and the 35 added between Nov 4 and Nov 12. Minimum plans, connector fees and prices come from the pair calculators; every figure there links to the vendor's pricing page, help center or developer docs, with the date it was checked (prices Nov 3 to Nov 8, 2026; the added pairs' requirements on Nov 4 and Nov 12).

**Baseline plans** (cheapest plan with API access, 10 seats): ActiveCampaign Starter ($15/mo); Airtable Free ($0/mo); Asana Starter ($134.90/mo); Calendly Free ($0/mo); ClickUp Free ($0/mo); Freshdesk Growth ($230/mo); HubSpot Free ($0/mo); Intercom Essential ($390/mo); Mailchimp Free ($0/mo); Monday.com Standard ($140/mo); Notion Plus ($120/mo); Pipedrive Lite ($140/mo); QuickBooks Simple Start ($38/mo); Salesforce Professional ($1,250/mo); Slack Free ($0/mo); Stripe Standard ($0/mo); Xero Early ($27/mo); Zendesk Suite Team ($690/mo).

**Limitations.** HubSpot Professional is modeled as Smart CRM Professional ($50 per seat a month, $45 on annual billing), the cheapest subscription that unlocks the Salesforce connector and the Asana workflow action; other Professional Hubs cost more. Make Core's $10.59 monthly price comes from third-party pricing reviews, because make.com blocks automated checks. Salesforce's baseline includes the $25/user API add-on that Professional needs. Stripe has no plan fee, so its baseline is $0. 5 integrations include an upgrade that rests on a plan requirement we haven't confirmed yet (Salesforce + Slack, Mailchimp + Airtable, ActiveCampaign + Pipedrive, HubSpot + ClickUp, HubSpot + Mailchimp), worth $803 a month in total; counting only confirmed requirements, 25 of 90 integrations force an upgrade instead of 28. Usage-based charges (Zapier tasks, Make credits, API overages) aren't included. On 14 pair pages the extra cost shown is lower than in this table: a pair's calculator starts from the cheapest plan it lists for each tool, and for Calendly, ClickUp, Mailchimp and Slack that is a paid plan unless the integration works on the free one, while this report always starts from the free plan.

**Why the headline is split.** Integrations that force a plan upgrade and those that only need a connector differ by an order of magnitude, so a single median across both swings between the two groups whenever one row changes. The figures are therefore reported per group: the range covers the middle half of the integrations in each group.

## Integration tax: frequently asked questions

### What is the integration tax?

It's the extra monthly cost of connecting two SaaS tools: any plan upgrade an integration forces above each tool's cheapest plan with API access, plus any connector fee for a service such as Make or Zapier. Across 90 popular integrations, 53 have one.

### How much does a SaaS integration cost per month?

It depends on whether the integration forces a plan upgrade. For a 10-person team, the 28 integrations here that force one usually add $120 to $320 a month (median $185.00), while the 25 that only need a connector add $10.59 to $29.99. 37 of 90 integrations cost nothing extra, and the most expensive, Intercom + Salesforce and Salesforce + Calendly, cost $600.00 a month.

### Is it cheaper to use Zapier or Make than a native integration?

It can be. Connector fees in this index are $10.59, $15, $29.99 a month, while forced plan upgrades make up 94% of the total integration tax, so when a native connector needs a higher plan and the middleware route works on a cheaper one, middleware usually wins. Each pair's calculator compares the native and middleware totals at your team size.
