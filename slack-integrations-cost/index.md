## Which Slack integrations force a plan upgrade?

1 of the 16 integrations needs a more expensive Slack plan than Free, Slack's cheapest plan with API access. The extra Slack cost at 10 seats: Salesforce + Slack needs Pro (+$87.50/mo). That gap is the hidden cost: you pay it every month even if you only wanted the integration. Each pair's "Hidden Cost Trap" box shows the full breakdown.

## Which Slack integrations need Make or Zapier?

2 of the 16 have no native connector and run through a third-party service: Xero + Slack (Make Core, $10.59/mo); QuickBooks + Slack (Zapier Professional, $29.99/mo). Connector fees are the monthly-billing price; the calculators use the lower annual-billing price (Make Core $9, Zapier Professional $19.99, Data Mapper $12.75) when you switch on annual billing. Real usage can push Make or Zapier onto a bigger plan, so check your expected volume.

## Slack integration costs: frequently asked questions

### Which Slack plan do I need for integrations?

Of the 16 Slack integrations in this index, 15 work on Slack's Free plan. The rest need a higher plan: Salesforce Sales Cloud needs Slack Pro. Each pair's calculator shows the exact minimum plan on both sides.

### Which Slack integrations need Zapier or Make?

2 of the 16 have no native connector: Xero (Make Core, $10.59/month); QuickBooks Online (Zapier Professional, $29.99/month).

### How much does a Slack integration cost for a 10-person team?

Including both tools' minimum plans and any connector fee, the 10-seat monthly totals range from $0.00 (Slack + Airtable) to $1,737.50 (Salesforce + Slack), at monthly-billing prices.
