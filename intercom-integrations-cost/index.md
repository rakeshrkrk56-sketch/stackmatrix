## Which Intercom integrations force a plan upgrade?

1 of the 7 integrations needs a more expensive Intercom plan than Essential, Intercom's cheapest plan with API access. The extra Intercom cost at 10 seats: Intercom + Salesforce needs Advanced (+$600.00/mo). That gap is the hidden cost: you pay it every month even if you only wanted the integration. Each pair's "Hidden Cost Trap" box shows the full breakdown.

## Which Intercom integrations need Make or Zapier?

None of the 7: every Intercom integration here has a native connector, so there's no Make or Zapier fee unless you choose one.

## Intercom integration costs: frequently asked questions

### Which Intercom plan do I need for integrations?

Of the 7 Intercom integrations in this index, 6 work on Intercom's Essential plan. The rest need a higher plan: Salesforce Sales Cloud needs Intercom Advanced. Each pair's calculator shows the exact minimum plan on both sides.

### Which Intercom integrations need Zapier or Make?

None of the 7 integrations here; each has a native connector.

### How much does a Intercom integration cost for a 10-person team?

Including both tools' minimum plans and any connector fee, the 10-seat monthly totals range from $390.00 (Intercom + Calendly) to $2,240.00 (Intercom + Salesforce), at monthly-billing prices.
