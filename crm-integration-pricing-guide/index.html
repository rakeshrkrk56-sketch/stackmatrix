The native vs middleware decision

Go native when…

  • Both tools already sit on tiers with API access
  • You need real-time sync at high volume
  • The middleware fee exceeds the tier difference

Go middleware when…

  • Native forces a big plan upgrade (typically $120–$251/mo at 10 seats in our research)
  • You connect 3+ tools in one workflow
  • Your team is small and per-seat jumps hurt most

CRM integration costs: frequently asked questions

Which CRM is cheapest to integrate: HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive?

In this index, HubSpot has the lowest floor: most of its integrations here work on the free CRM, although the Salesforce and Asana ones need HubSpot Professional. Pipedrive's integrations start on its Lite plan ($14/seat, billed annually), and Slack alerts need its Growth plan. Salesforce is the most expensive, because API access needs Professional plus a $25/user add-on, or Enterprise for Slack.

When does Zapier or Make beat a native CRM integration on cost?

When the native path forces a plan upgrade that costs more than the middleware fee. This index models Make Core at $10.59/month ($9 billed annually) and Zapier Professional at $29.99/month ($19.99 billed annually), so even a small per-seat upgrade across a team usually costs more.
