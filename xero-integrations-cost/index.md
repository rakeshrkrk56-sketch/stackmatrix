## Does Xero force an upgrade?

Not for any of the 10 integrations here: each one works on Xero's cheapest plan with API access, so the integration itself adds no Xero upgrade cost. Any extra cost comes from the other tool's plan or from a connector fee, shown in the table above.

## Which Xero integrations need Make or Zapier?

8 of the 10 have no native connector and run through a third-party service: Xero + Airtable (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Xero + Slack (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Xero + ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Xero + Asana (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Xero + Calendly (Zapier Professional, $29.99/mo); Pipedrive + Xero (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Xero + Zendesk (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Salesforce + Xero (Make Core, $10.59/mo). Connector fees are the monthly-billing price; the calculators use the lower annual-billing price (Make Core $9, Zapier Professional $19.99, Data Mapper $12.75) when you switch on annual billing. Real usage can push Make or Zapier onto a bigger plan, so check your expected volume.

## Xero integration costs: frequently asked questions

### Which Xero plan do I need for integrations?

Of the 10 Xero integrations in this index, 10 work on Xero's Early plan. None needs a higher plan. Each pair's calculator shows the exact minimum plan on both sides.

### Which Xero integrations need Zapier or Make?

8 of the 10 have no native connector: Airtable (Make Core, $10.59/month); Slack (Make Core, $10.59/month); ActiveCampaign (Make Core, $10.59/month); Asana (Make Core, $10.59/month); Calendly (Zapier Professional, $29.99/month); Pipedrive (Make Core, $10.59/month); Zendesk (Make Core, $10.59/month); Salesforce Sales Cloud (Make Core, $10.59/month).

### How much does a Xero integration cost for a 10-person team?

Including both tools' minimum plans and any connector fee, the 10-seat monthly totals range from $27.00 (HubSpot + Xero) to $1,287.59 (Salesforce + Xero), at monthly-billing prices.
