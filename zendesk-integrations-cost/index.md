## Does Zendesk force an upgrade?

Not for any of the 9 integrations here: each one works on Zendesk's cheapest plan with API access, so the integration itself adds no Zendesk upgrade cost. Any extra cost comes from the other tool's plan or from a connector fee, shown in the table above.

## Which Zendesk integrations need Make or Zapier?

2 of the 9 have no native connector and run through a third-party service: Zendesk + Stripe (Make Core, $10.59/mo); Zendesk + Notion (Zapier Professional, $29.99/mo). Connector fees are the monthly-billing price; the calculators use the lower annual-billing price (Make Core $9, Zapier Professional $19.99, Data Mapper $12.75) when you switch on annual billing. Real usage can push Make or Zapier onto a bigger plan, so check your expected volume.

## Zendesk integration costs: frequently asked questions

### Which Zendesk plan do I need for integrations?

Of the 9 Zendesk integrations in this index, 9 work on Zendesk's Suite Team plan. None needs a higher plan. Each pair's calculator shows the exact minimum plan on both sides.

### Which Zendesk integrations need Zapier or Make?

2 of the 9 have no native connector: Stripe (Make Core, $10.59/month); Notion (Zapier Professional, $29.99/month).

### How much does a Zendesk integration cost for a 10-person team?

Including both tools' minimum plans and any connector fee, the 10-seat monthly totals range from $690.00 (ClickUp + Zendesk) to $1,940.00 (Salesforce + Zendesk), at monthly-billing prices.
